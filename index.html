About Me

Business Analyst by Experience. Product Thinker by Mindset.

I'm a Senior Business Analyst with 6+ years in Agile/Scrum environments, having worked across Document management system, contract management system, fleet management, e-commerce, supply chain, healthcare EHR, wealth management, and BFSI cards — different domains, same core skill of turning complexity into clarity.

My focus is turning ambiguous stakeholder asks into decisions a team can actually build against: weighing what users need against what engineering can realistically ship, and using AI to speed up documentation and edge-case analysis so I can spend more time on the calls that matter.

"Every decision I make starts with the user, not the backlog."

A few examples of my thinking in practice, not just theory:

  • ✔ I say no to good ideas so the team has room for the few great ones — prioritization is subtraction, not addition.
  • ✔ I look for the smallest version of a solution that tests the riskiest assumption, rather than building the full vision on day one.
  • ✔ When a metric moves, I ask what user behavior actually caused it before I act on it.
  • ✔ I write the one-pager before I write the spec — if I can't explain why it matters in a page, it's not ready to build.
  • ✔ I treat launch as the start of learning, not the end of the project — the real work is what we do with what happens after.

Insights

AI in Business Analysis

How artificial intelligence is transforming the BA role from manual documentation to high-value problem solving.

01

Requirement Gathering

How can AI make requirement gathering better?

Traditional BA

Spends significant time collecting requirements through meetings, interviews, emails, and documents, then manually organizes the information.

BA using AI

Uses AI to summarize meetings, identify recurring requirements, group similar needs, and highlight missing or unclear information.

Use Case

A BA conducts five stakeholder meetings for a new application.

Instead of manually going through every meeting note, AI can help create a consolidated requirement list. The BA then validates it with stakeholders.

Key Idea

AI reduces the documentation effort. The BA still owns the understanding.

02

Finding Business Problems

Can AI help a BA find problems that people don't immediately notice?

Traditional BA

Relies heavily on stakeholder conversations, observations, reports, and manually analyzing available information.

BA using AI

Can use AI to identify patterns, unusual behavior, repeated complaints, and potential problem areas across large amounts of information.

Use Case

A company receives thousands of customer complaints.

Instead of reading every complaint individually, AI can group them into themes such as: Payment issues, Login problems, Delivery delays, Product usability.

The BA can then investigate the biggest underlying problems.

Key Idea

AI can find patterns. The BA decides what those patterns mean.

03

Writing Requirements

Can AI write requirements for a Business Analyst?

Traditional BA

Creates user stories, acceptance criteria, process flows, and documentation manually based on gathered requirements.

BA using AI

Uses AI as a first draft assistant to convert notes into user stories, suggest acceptance criteria, identify gaps, and improve clarity.

Use Case

A stakeholder says: "Users should be able to reset their password."

AI can help turn this into a structured user story and suggest possible scenarios. But the BA still needs to validate:

  • Who can reset the password?
  • What verification is required?
  • What happens after multiple failed attempts?
  • What are the business rules?
Key Idea

AI can write the first draft. The BA makes it correct.

04

Data Analysis

What happens when a BA has too much data and too little time?

Traditional BA

Uses spreadsheets, dashboards, SQL, and reports to analyze data and manually search for trends.

BA using AI

Can use AI to explore datasets faster, identify trends, generate initial summaries, and suggest questions worth investigating.

Use Case

A product team notices that customer cancellations have increased.

Instead of looking at hundreds of rows manually, AI can help identify patterns such as: Cancellations → specific customer segment → specific product feature → specific period.

The BA then validates the finding using actual business data.

Key Idea

AI helps you get to the question faster. Analysis still requires human judgment.

05

From Problem to Solution

Does AI change how a BA approaches solutions?

Traditional BA

Analyzes the problem, gathers requirements, evaluates possible solutions, and works with stakeholders to define the best approach.

BA using AI

Can explore more solution possibilities quickly, compare approaches, create prototypes, and test assumptions before investing heavily in development.

Use Case

A business wants to reduce the time employees spend answering repetitive questions.

A BA using AI could quickly explore several options: Knowledge base → Search → Chatbot → AI assistant → Process automation.

Rather than immediately choosing the most exciting option, the BA compares each solution against the actual business problem.

Key Idea

AI gives you more options. Good BA thinking helps you choose the right one.

06

Process Analysis

How can AI help a BA understand a complex business process?

Traditional BA

Maps the process through stakeholder interviews, observations, documents, and manual analysis.

BA using AI

Uses AI to summarize process documentation, identify repeated steps, spot possible bottlenecks, and compare the current process with the desired process.

Use Case

A purchase process has 15 steps and involves three departments.

AI can help identify that two teams are collecting the same information at different stages.

The BA investigates whether that duplication can be removed.

Key Idea

AI helps you see the process faster. The BA decides what should change.

07

Stakeholder Communication

Can AI help a BA communicate with different stakeholders?

Traditional BA

Creates different versions of requirements, presentations, emails, and documentation for different audiences.

BA using AI

Uses AI to simplify technical information, create stakeholder-friendly summaries, and adjust communication based on the audience.

Use Case

The development team needs detailed requirements. A business stakeholder wants a simple explanation of the same feature.

AI can help create both versions—but the BA ensures that the meaning remains accurate.

Key Idea

Same requirement. Different audience. Clearer communication.

08

Identifying Requirement Gaps

What if an important requirement was never discussed?

Traditional BA

Uses experience, stakeholder discussions, process analysis, and checklists to identify missing requirements.

BA using AI

Can ask AI to review requirements and highlight possible missing scenarios, dependencies, exceptions, or edge cases.

Use Case

A requirement says: "Users can cancel an order."

AI might prompt the BA to consider:

  • What if the order has already shipped?
  • What if payment has already been processed?
  • What happens to a refund?
  • Who can cancel the order?

The BA then validates these scenarios with the business.

Key Idea

AI can ask "What about...?" The BA decides whether it matters.

09

Impact Analysis

What happens when one requirement changes?

Traditional BA

Manually checks related requirements, workflows, teams, systems, and documents to understand the impact.

BA using AI

Can use AI to quickly identify related requirements, dependencies, documents, and potential areas affected by the change.

Use Case

A business decides to change a customer verification rule.

That small change may affect: Login → Registration → Payments → Notifications → Reports.

AI can help identify the areas to investigate. The BA validates the impact before the team starts development.

Key Idea

AI helps connect the dots. The BA understands the consequences.

10

Testing & Acceptance Criteria

Can AI help a BA think about what could go wrong?

Traditional BA

Creates acceptance criteria and test scenarios based on requirements and business rules.

BA using AI

Uses AI to generate possible positive, negative, and edge-case scenarios from the requirements.

Use Case

Requirement: "A user can upload a document."

AI can help suggest scenarios such as: Valid file → Invalid file → Large file → Wrong format → Missing file.

The BA then checks which scenarios actually apply to the business.

Key Idea

AI can generate possibilities. The BA validates the business reality.
